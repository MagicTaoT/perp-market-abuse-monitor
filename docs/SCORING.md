# Perp Market Abuse Monitor: Structural Scoring v2

Updated 2026-10-23. This is a research ranking for possible crowding, forced covering, and supply pressure. It does not identify an operator or prove manipulation. The calculation is read-only and never places trades.

## Data alignment

- The market universe is checked every 15 minutes. Price and token-denominated OI changes use the **same exchange and contract** at approximately 1, 4, and 24 hours earlier, accepting observations within 18 minutes of each target. A missing baseline stays unknown. The ranking retains only contracts with verified current OI of at least 1 million USDT equivalent and a recent price/OI pair.
- Bybit documents OI as including both sides. For supply ratios the engine divides its reported token OI and nominal USD OI by two. This is a **single venue, single side observation**, not the whole market. KuCoin contract count is normalized into tokens but its side-count convention is not reconciled. Binance's broad reply has no OI. The dashboard therefore reports a Bybit observation and coverage label rather than adding incompatible numbers into a false total.
- CoinMarketCap's [keyless public API](https://coinmarketcap.com/api/documentation/pro-api-reference/keyless-public-api) provides CMC ID, circulating supply, market cap, FDV and price. Ticker collisions stay unresolved; an explicitly verified CMC ID is required for BMT. Supply/valuation older than two days, or with a stale source timestamp, is unknown. Supply is reported circulating supply, not audited freely tradable float.
- CMC's ID map can reject a whole symbol batch when one exchange ticker is absent. The collector splits HTTP 400 batches to isolate invalid tickers, retaining valid identities; rate limits and network failures do not trigger extra split requests. Rejected and ambiguous tickers remain unknown.
- `OI / float % = (Bybit OI tokens / 2) / CMC circulating tokens × 100`. `OI / FDV % = (Bybit OI tokens / 2) / (CMC FDV USD / CMC price USD) × 100`. The second denominator is the FDV-equivalent token count, so both sides use the same token unit. CMC FDV and market cap dollars appear separately as context. Price mismatch across sources does not manufacture an OI increase.
- The daily [PatternPlus unlock file](https://pattern.plus/developers) supplies dated events, amount and percent of circulating supply for only a subset of projects. Symbol **and project name/slug** must match CMC identity. An observed upcoming event can be scored; absent coverage is **unknown**, never "no unlock". The source requires a linked `Data: PatternPlus` attribution wherever its data is displayed.
- The settlement interval comes from Bybit's [instrument metadata](https://bybit-exchange.github.io/docs/v5/market/instrument) in minutes or KuCoin's [active-contract metadata](https://www.kucoin.com/en-au/docs-new/rest/futures-trading/market-data/get-all-symbols) in milliseconds. If the interval is absent or old, funding cost and funding-related score components remain unknown. Binance interval is not assumed to be eight hours.

## Four independent tracks

Every track has 100 possible points. An item contributes points only when all of its inputs are available and pass unit/freshness checks. The API returns `score` only when all 100 possible points are covered. Otherwise it reports an observed lower bound, maximum possible range, missing inputs and coverage. A missing unlock schedule or history never silently contributes zero.

Selected multi-level bands: OI/float at 1, 3, 7, 15 and 30%; OI/FDV at 0.25, 0.5, 1 and 3%; observed single-venue OI at 1, 5, 20 and 100 million USD; OI growth 1h at 2, 5, 10 and 20%; 4h at 5, 10, 20 and 40%; 24h at 10, 20, 40 and 80%. Funding is standardized to an eight-hour equivalent; negative/current bands are −5, −10, −25 and −50 basis points, with mirrored positive bands and separate acceleration bands. The unlock component combines size at 1, 3, 7 and 15% of circulating supply with time bands at 30, 14, 7 and 2 days. Exact point assignments and all remaining bands are in [`structural_scoring.py`](../gold_basis_mm_bot/squeeze_engine/structural_scoring.py).

Completed opportunity tiers are `<25 low`, `25–49 watch`, `50–74 elevated`, `75–100 high`; risk tiers are `<25 low`, `25–49 moderate`, `50–74 high`, `75–100 severe`. Incomplete tracks have no tier. A fully covered early track at 75+ can become a research candidate. A high elevated short watch becomes a candidate only when a fully covered short-risk track is below 50. A confirmed lower bound of 40+ with at least 50% coverage is shown as a **lead requiring verification**; it is not a complete score or trade signal. Other cases remain observation rows, including a strong price move with unknown short carrying cost.

The broad scan checks all eligible contracts. A bounded structural prefilter then takes the largest recent price/OI moves, absolute OI, extreme funding, OI/float ratios when available, and upcoming matched unlocks. The dashboard reports how many contracts passed this prefilter; it does not claim to compute the full structural model on every market. Within that set the shortlist is sorted by the **confirmed evidence lower bound** of the two opportunity tracks, with the earlier price/OI rank breaking ties. This is a triage order, not a probability. Because data coverage differs, the score range and coverage must be read together. A new installation needs about seven days for a funding percentile and twenty-one days for its 21-day price/OI percentiles; before then complete tiers will be unavailable.

## Funding holding cost

For a position with notional `N` and current quoted funding rate `f` per settlement period of `h` hours: a long has estimated next-settlement funding `−N × f`; a short has `+N × f`. The eight-hour equivalent rate is `f × 8 / h`. The displayed simple annualized **scenario** is `± f × 24 / h × 365 × 100%`, assuming an unchanged rate and position. Fees, slippage, liquidation, and changing prices are outside that one-line carry scenario. The quoted current funding is not a history of payments actually received.

Funding changes are absolute basis-point differences from the same contract one and four hours earlier. The seven-day signed percentile compares the contract with its own observed history, not an arbitrary global rate. If the observed funding interval changed within that history, the percentile is unknown.

## Collection and replay

The example configuration collects the broad market every 15 minutes, IRYS detail every five minutes, and at most 75 shortlisted or large-OI CMC identities plus one unlock calendar once per day. The daily due time persists across process restarts. A failed supply collection retries after one hour; a 429 is logged and the dashboard can still use the last successful scan while it remains under 36 hours old. Raw replies, source timestamps, receipt timestamps and normalized observations are retained. The unlock calendar is stored with its receipt time, so an `as_of` query cannot use a later calendar. No API key is required.
